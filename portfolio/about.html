My story

I'm Your Name, a Computer Science student at Your University in Kampala, Uganda. I started programming because I wanted to understand how the apps I use every day actually work, and I haven't stopped asking that question since.

My first real breakthrough was writing a Java program that modelled a bank account, complete with custom exceptions for failed withdrawals. Seeing my own code handle mistakes gracefully made software feel like engineering, not just typing.
